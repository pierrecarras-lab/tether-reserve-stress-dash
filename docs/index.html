Model notes

Assumptions and source links

Assumptions

  • Liabilities stay fixed at the official December 31, 2025 reported amount.
  • BTC units are implied from Tether's disclosed BTC value and BTC price in the report.
  • Gold ounces are implied from Tether's disclosed gold value and the December 31, 2025 XAU close used as a proxy.
  • Everything outside BTC and gold remains marked at reported values unless you move it via the haircut table mentally.
  • No newer official reserve report was listed on Tether's Data page when this dashboard was updated.

Sources

  • Tether Data page, which currently lists the latest official reserve report
  • Tether Q1 2025 attestation release, published May 1, 2025
  • Tether Q2 2025 attestation release, published July 31, 2025
  • Tether Q3 2025 attestation release, published October 31, 2025
  • Tether Q4 2025 attestation release, published January 30, 2026
  • Tether Financial Figures & Reserves Report, December 31, 2025
  • Live BTC price feed
  • Live XAU price feed
  • December 31, 2025 gold close proxy used for implied ounces
